## Act III — Notes, chat, call (~0:26–0:48)

**Benefit story:** Bring your lesson notes (Google Doc link or upload). Honza treats them as memory. He coaches you on **that** Czech in text and on a **call** so you retain and improve what you already learned. Closing line = why that matters for you (confidence, next lesson, curiosity).

**Visual (Step B):** Doc link/upload → knedlíky line highlighted → chat beat from notes → full **Call** UI, mic live, same thread.
