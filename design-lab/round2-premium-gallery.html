Three ways to make Honza feel expensive.

Round 1 was quiet-editorial and read as spartan. This round targets the other kind of premium: rich, dimensional, alive. Each direction below is a complete option you could ship, and Classic stays untouched as the fallback. No mascots, no streaks, no invented numbers.
